Worksheets

Print these or copy them into a notebook. Fill-in regions are shown with underscores or checkbox rows. No accounts, no network, no cost, ever.

Worksheets pair with chapters: Energy Ledger with Energy Accounting, slices with Task Slicing, and so on.

Worksheet 1: The Daily Energy Ledger

One per day. Copy this block into a notebook, or print a page of blanks.

DATE: ____ / ____ / ______     BATTERY TODAY: ____ %

TOLL LIST (costs more energy than its minutes suggest):
  1. ________________________________
  2. ________________________________

TODAY'S PLAN (keep total under ~80% of battery):
  ANCHOR TASK (one thing that makes today a win):
    ______________________________________________
  SLICES (1-3, pre-sliced when possible):
    1. ____________________________________________
    2. ____________________________________________
  REST SLOT (time + chosen rest):
    ____:____   ____________________________________

ACTUALS (fill at night, one line each, no commentary):
  anchor done?  [ ] yes  [ ] 2-min version  [ ] parked
  slices done?  [ ] yes  [ ] 2-min versions  [ ] parked
  rest happened? [ ] yes  [ ] kind of  [ ] no
  One word for the day: ____________

  (dump the mental version on paper, messy is fine)

  _______________________________________________________
  _______________________________________________________

  1. _________________________________________________
  2. _________________________________________________
  3. _________________________________________________
  4. _________________________________________________

SLICE 1 only:
  2-minute version: ___________________________________
  Where do you start standing? ________________________

  "I will do slice 1. After slice 1, I may stop, with full
   credit for a successful day."  Sign: ________________

High-cost tasks become the first thing to delegate, slice down, or replace with a 2-minute version. Low-cost tasks are your "free wins" — bank them for streak days.

Worksheet 5: The Restart Ritual Card

Full instructions in Restart Rituals. Print. Cut along the line. Keep one visible.

THE RESTART RITUAL ---------- 10 minutes ----
1. SAY:    "I crashed. That's allowed. I'm restarting."
2. AMNESTY: old list is dead, not owed.
3. RESET:  one landing action (trash bag / dishes
           into machine / off the bed).
4. ANCHOR: enter ONE anchor task in the planner,
           priced for today's battery.
5. LATER, if a good day comes up:
           "What cost more energy than usual?"

Worksheet 6: Tell People Your Truth (body doubling offer script)

WHAT I'M ASKING: just be in the room (or on a call),
doing your thing, while I do mine.

WHAT I'M NOT ASKING: help, advice, watching,
timing me, or any commentary.

WHEN IT HELPS: whenever I say "can you be my double
for 10 minutes?"

SCRIPT I WILL SAY OUT LOUD:
  _________________________________________________

Printing and reuse

  • Print one page of Worksheet 1 (or 7 copies for a week).
  • Worksheets 2, 4, 6 are one-time setups — refill when they teach you something new.
  • Worksheet 5 is the emergency card: whenever it gets used and crumpled, print a new one. Crumple = a crash that you restarted, which is a win with creases.
